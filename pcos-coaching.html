Questions

Frequently asked

Can I lose weight with PCOS?

Yes. Fat loss with PCOS is possible with the right mix of nutrition, strength training, sleep and consistency. It can be slower than usual, which is why the plan is built around your body, your routine and your food.

Do I have to give up rice or roti?

No. We don't ban the foods you grew up with. We adjust portions and pair them with protein and vegetables, so your meals keep you full and your energy steady.

Can couples join for fertility coaching?

Yes. For couples trying to conceive, both partners can work on nutrition and lifestyle together, alongside the advice of your doctor.

When can I start after having a baby?

Once your doctor has cleared you for exercise. We start gently, rebuild strength step by step, and plan around your sleep and your baby's routine.

Is coaching online or in person?

Both. Coaching is available online anywhere in India, and in person in Bengaluru.
