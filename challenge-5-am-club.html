Your guided plan

The day-by-day guide

Tap a week to see exactly what each day asks of you.

Week1

Prepare

Days 1-7 · Shift your wake-up time gradually.
▾
  1. Day 1Write why you want 5 AM mornings and set tonight's bedtime (aim for 7-8 hours of sleep).
  2. Day 2Evening wind-down: screens off 30 minutes before bed. Lay out your clothes and water.
  3. Day 3Wake 30 minutes earlier than usual and drink water first thing.
  4. Day 4Wake 45 minutes earlier and get daylight within 10 minutes.
  5. Day 5Wake 1 hour earlier and do 5 minutes of stretching.
  6. Day 6Plan a simple 3-step morning routine.
  7. Day 7Check-in: how are your sleep and energy?
Week2

Build

Days 8-14 · Reach your wake-up time and add a routine.
▾
  1. Day 8Wake at 5:00 AM (or your chosen time). Drink water and stretch for 5 minutes.
  2. Day 9Add a 10-minute walk or movement.
  3. Day 10Add 5 quiet minutes: journaling, planning or breathing.
  4. Day 11No phone for the first 20 minutes after waking.
  5. Day 12Prepare a healthy breakfast the night before.
  6. Day 13Easy day: a gentle routine and an early bedtime.
  7. Day 14Check-in: what's the hardest part of waking up?
Week3

Own it

Days 15-21 · Make it a habit you love.
▾
  1. Day 15Do your full routine: wake, water, 15 minutes of movement and 5 quiet minutes.
  2. Day 16Add one thing just for you: reading, a hobby or a goal task (10 minutes).
  3. Day 17Write down your top 3 tasks for the day each morning.
  4. Day 18Keep your wake-up time even after a short night, and rest later if you need to.
  5. Day 19Share a morning photo with the group or invite a friend to join you.
  6. Day 20Notice what has changed in your mood, focus and energy.
  7. Day 21Final check-in: design the morning routine you'll keep.

Questions

Good to know

When does it start?

After you join, you're added to the next batch's WhatsApp group and Coach Sherni sends you the start date and first task there.

What if I miss a day?

Just pick it back up the next day. If you miss 2 days in a row, Coach Sherni will message you personally.

Do I need a gym or any equipment?

No. Everything is designed to be done at home with what you already have.

Can I join if I have a health condition or I'm pregnant?

These challenges are lifestyle support, not medical treatment. Please check with your doctor first if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or are on medication.
